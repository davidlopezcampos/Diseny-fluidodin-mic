## Team's work regulations:

1. The team will meet regularly, preferably once a week, to check the progress of the project, divide the tasks and discuss any issues that may come up.
2. Each team member will be in charge of the tasks related to their assigned role: Design, CFD or Experimentation.
3. Important decisions should be discussed together and agreed on by the whole team.
4. Everyone will keep the rest of the team updated on their progress, any problems they are facing and the results they obtain.
5. Relevant calculations, simulations, decisions and conclusions from meetings will be recorded in the project logbook.
6. Everyone will contribute to maintaining the GitHub repository and keeping the project documentation updated.
7. The deadlines agreed by the team should be respected. If someone expects to have a delay or has a problem, they should let the rest of the team know.
8. Before considering any important result or technical work as final, it should be reviewed by the rest of the team.
